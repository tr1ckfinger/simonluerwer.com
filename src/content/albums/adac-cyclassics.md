Race day in Hamburg. It is a flat course through the north - no hills mean there is not a lot of opportunities for the bulk of drivers to break up. The result is a lot of crashes that make this race a bit of a controversial one in the road racing community. Nevertheless it is

The pros however ride a different track. They have to climb Hamburgs Waseberg five times. It is a hard climb and i’m not sure whether the support from the fans can make up for it. No denying though that it is impressive to see the riders conquer this hill with a true display of force.

The last time they sprint up the hill opens up the race to the finish line. The peloton will not break until a couple hundred meters to the finish line - at which point it is Paul Magnier who wins by two bike-lengths.
