There is something about race day that is hard to put into words - the energy, the excitement that is shared between athletes, supporters and staff.

With an ironman that excitement is brought to the absolute extreme. Getting up at 4 in the morning to make last minute preparations and arrive a little bit early at the venue where we can already see everyone warming up for the swim.

Year after year the iconic Jungfernstieg is hosting the ultra endurance event. It is kicked off with a swim in the Alster - which is only one of two occasions this is allowed. Anyone around the sport knows that swimming is the most hated discipline amongst the three. Luckily its also the fastest.

Afterwards it is onto the bikes - 180km on a circuit through the outskirts of Hamburg. A moment to breathe for the many spectators that showed up. But not too long - everyone is still ready to cheer when Laura Philipp makes her rounds as the first one in the field.
This year its the pro women who can try and book the ticket to the Ironman world championship in Kona.

Unfortunately there is vandalism along the bike course. Not everyone is happy that roads are blocked for the day. Cars can’t get through and a few resort to violence - throwing metal spikes onto the road. Ending the race early for some of the competitors that are left stranded 30-40 kilometers outside of Hamburg.
On a day that features so many celebrations of others feats, it is a sad reminder of how petty we can sometimes be.

Back at the Jungfernstieg however this is only distant news. Those who finished the circuit are changing into their running gear and heading out on the track to finish this day with a bloody marathon.
