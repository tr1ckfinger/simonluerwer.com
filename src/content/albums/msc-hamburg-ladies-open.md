A tennis match is like an intricate, well coordinated dance. Ball boys and girls, stewards and the audience all settled in and ready to be entertained by two people who mastered their craft.

No TV camera could ever capture the raw power of the ball being served at 190 km/h, the intense moment of focus that each athlete summons right before. You have to be in the stadium, next to the court to experience this spectacle.

This year however the audience was swayed to favor one woman more than any other: Tamara Korpatsch was not only on a inspiring run - she is also from Hamburg. Her opponent, Anna Bondár, who had finaled the previous two years, took it in stride.

In a thrilling finale it was Korpatsch who got the better start into the match. Having the edge in multiple close exchanges, she was able to carry that momentum until the end - giving the crowd what they were hoping for: a hometown champion.
