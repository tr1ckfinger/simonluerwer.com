date: "July 11–12, 2026"
location: "Hamburg, Germany"
---

About five weeks after the Ironman athletes captivated the city it’s time for an even bigger Triathlon experience. This weekend athletes of all ages and levels will race through Hamburg.

One thing that the event is famous for is having the worlds longest transition zone of around 550m - making the unofficial “fourth discipline” especially challenging for competitors.

They are also cheered on by between 250,000 to 500,000 viewers and fans. The Jungfernstieg is buzzing with energy and excitement.

Saturday evening it is the turn of the pros who race at an exhilarating pace - seeing them dash across the town hall square is a firm reminder of what the human body is capable of.
This evening is a special affair for Matthew Hauser (AUS) and Leonie Periault (FRA) who win their respective races. Lisa Tertsch (GER), the local hero, comes into second place just five seconds behind the frenchwoman.

Next year Hamburg will host the World Triathlon Championship Finals on September 8th-12th.
